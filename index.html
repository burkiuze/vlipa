Process

How a project actually runs.

No black box. You know what is being built, what it costs and what state it is in, from the first conversation to the version in production.

01

Discovery

We sit with the people doing the work, follow the process as it is today and write down where it breaks.

02

Scope

What is in, what is out, how long and how much. Signed off before anyone opens an editor.

03

Build

Two-week rounds, each ending in something you can click. Direction changes here cost nothing.

04

Launch & care

Migration, training, handover. Then monitoring, updates and the fixes that come after real use.

Principles

Four things we do not bend on.

Scope before estimates.

We write down what is being built and what is not, then put a price on it. Anything outside that is priced before the work starts, not after.

Working software every two weeks.

You see running software throughout, not a demo at the end. Problems surface while they are still cheap to fix.

Everything is yours.

Source code, servers and accounts are in your name from the first day. Nothing is built to keep you dependent on us.

We stay after launch.

Maintenance, updates and the occasional call at an inconvenient hour are part of the work, not a separate conversation.
