How we work

Scope before estimates.

We write down what is being built and what is not, then put a price on it. Anything outside that is priced before the work starts, not after.

Working software every two weeks.

You see running software throughout, not a demo at the end. Direction is easier to change early.

Everything is yours.

Source code, servers and accounts are in your name from the first day. Nothing is built to keep you dependent on us.

We stay after launch.

Maintenance, updates and the occasional call at an inconvenient hour are part of the work.

Studio

Small team, long projects, few clients at a time.

Design, engineering, infrastructure and data sit in one team, so responsibility never gets passed around. Every project runs with the same people from the first sketch to the version in production.
